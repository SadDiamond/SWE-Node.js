    Reading the graph

    Spot onWay off

    • ● Dot: a spot that's on the curve, which you get from a hint.
    • ▲ Arrow on an edge: the curve leaves the graph here.
    • → Arrow on your line: you're way off here and the curve is in that direction.
    • Grey bars on the edges: you missed the curve somewhere above the bar, or somewhere level with it.
    • Grey dots: your line joins two pieces of the curve that aren't connected.
    Drawing with a keyboard

    Tab to the graph and use the arrow keys to move the pen around. Press Space to put it down, then press Space again when you want to lift it and finish the stroke.

      How to play

      1. Find the hidden curve. There's a curve hidden on the graph and your job is to work out where it goes. Pick how hard you want them at the top and keep going for as long as you like. The little arrows on the edges show where the curve goes off the graph, so they're a good place to start.
      2. Draw your guess. Draw the curve with your mouse or finger and it gets marked as soon as you let go. If the curve is split into pieces, you draw each piece as its own line. You only get so much ink, so you can't just scribble over the whole graph.
      3. See how close you were. Your line turns green where it's on the curve and goes red and dashed where it's off, and wherever you're way off a small arrow points you towards the real curve.
      4. Fix it up. You get 5 attempts per curve. Each one is a fresh drawing, but your last one stays faded on the graph so you can trace over the good bits and fix the rest. Get 85% or more and you've cracked it. If you're stuck, your first hint tells you what kind of curve it is and the next ones show you spots on it.
      5. Scoring. Each curve is out of 1000 depending on how close you get, and you lose 100 for every extra attempt and 150 for every hint you use. Scores never go below 0.
