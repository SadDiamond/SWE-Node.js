    How it works

    Spot onWay off

    • Your line stays thick and green where you're on the curve and gets thinner and redder the further off you are.
    • ● Dot: a spot that's on the curve, which you can get from a hint if you're stuck (it'll cost you some points though).
    • Scoring: each curve is out of 1000 depending on how accurate you are, and you lose 100 for every extra attempt and 150 for every hint.
    • ▲ Arrow on the edge: the curve leaves the graph here.
    • → Arrow on your line: you're way off here and the curve is in that direction.
    • Grey bar at the bottom: the curve is somewhere above it and you didn't get anywhere near.
    • Ink: you get enough to draw the whole curve with a bit left over.
    • Your last attempt stays on the graph, so you can trace over the good bits.
    Drawing with a keyboard

    Tab to the graph and use the arrow keys to move the pen around. Press Space to put it down, then press Space again when you want to lift it and finish the stroke.
