      How to play

      1. Find the hidden curve. There's a curve hidden on the graph and your job is to work out where it goes. Each day has three of them, starting easy and getting harder. The little arrows on the edges show where the curve goes off the graph, so they're a good place to start.
      2. Draw your guess. Draw the curve with your mouse or finger and it gets marked as soon as you let go. If the curve is split into pieces, you draw each piece as its own line. You only get so much ink, so you can't just scribble over the whole graph.
      3. See how close you were. Your line turns green where it's on the curve and red where it's off, and wherever you're way off a small arrow points you towards the real curve.
      4. Fix it up. You get 5 attempts per curve and your last one stays on the graph, so you can keep the good bits and redraw the rest. Get 85% or more and you've cracked it. If you're stuck, a hint shows you one spot that's on the curve.
      5. Scoring. Each curve is out of 1000 depending on how close you get, and you lose 100 for every extra attempt and 150 for every hint you use.
