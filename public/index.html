    Graph

      Type of curve:

      The black line is the real curve, with your last attempt faded behind it, and the dashed lines are asymptotes.

      Reading your line

      Spot onWay off

      • Your line is thick and green where you're on the curve, and gets thinner and redder the further off you are.
      • Red can also mean there's no curve in that spot at all.
      • ● Dot: a spot on the curve. You get these from hints.
      • Scoring: each curve is out of 1000 depending on how close you get. You lose 100 for each extra attempt and 150 for each hint, and scores never go below 0.
      • ▲ Arrow on an edge: where the curve leaves the graph.
      • → Arrow on your line: you're way off here, and the curve is that way.
      • Grey bar along the bottom: part of the curve you haven't drawn yet. Look above the bar.
      • Your last attempt stays faded on the graph, so you can trace over the good bits.
      • You can draw any shape you like, even loops and lines that go straight up and down.
      Drawing with a keyboard

      Tab to the graph and use the arrow keys to move the pen. Press Space to put the pen down, move around to draw, then press Space again to lift it and finish the stroke.
