    Reading the graph

    Spot onWay off

    • ● Dot: a spot on the curve. You get these from hints, and a loop that never reaches the edges starts with one.
    • ▲ Arrow on an edge: where the curve leaves the graph.
    • → Arrow on your line: you're way off here, and the curve is that way.
    • Grey bars on the edges: part of the curve you haven't drawn yet. Look above the bars on the bottom edge and across from the ones on the left.
    • Grey dots: your line joins two pieces of the curve that aren't connected.
    Drawing with a keyboard

    Tab to the graph and move the pen with the arrow keys. Press Space to put it down and Space again to lift it.

      How to play

      1. Find the hidden curve. There's a curve hidden on the graph and you have to work out where it goes. Choose a difficulty at the top, or Any for a mix. The arrows on the edges show where it leaves the graph, which makes them a good place to start.
      2. Draw your guess. Draw it with your mouse or finger and it gets marked when you let go. If the curve comes in pieces, draw each piece as its own line. You only get so much ink, so you can't just scribble over the whole graph.
      3. See how close you were. Your line turns green where it's on the curve and red and dashed where it's off. Where you're way off, a small arrow points towards the real curve.
      4. Fix it up. You get 5 attempts per curve. Each attempt is a new drawing, but the last one stays faded on the graph for you to trace over. Get 85% or more and you've cracked it. If you're stuck, the first hint tells you the type of curve and the next two show you spots on it.
      5. Scoring. Each curve is out of 1000 depending on how close you get. You lose 100 for each extra attempt and 150 for each hint, and scores never go below 0.
