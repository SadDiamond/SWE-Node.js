      How it works

      Spot onWay off

      • Green and thick means you're on the curve. Thinner and redder means you're further off.
      • ● Dot: a spot on the curve. Hints give you one, but they cost points.
      • Scoring: out of 1000, based on accuracy. Each extra go is −100, each hint −150.
      • ▲ Arrow on the edge: the curve leaves the graph here.
      • → Arrow on your line: you're way off here. The curve is that way.
      • Grey bar at the bottom: you missed the curve somewhere above it.
      • Ink: enough to draw the curve, plus a bit extra.
      • Your last go stays on the graph, so you can trace over the good bits.
      Drawing with a keyboard

      Tab to the graph. Arrow keys move the pen. Space puts it down, Space again lifts it and finishes the stroke.
